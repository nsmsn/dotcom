ZieherSmith gallery has a great looking baseball-themed show up called [Augie Garrido or Gonfalons for the Grand Old Game](https://www.ziehersmith.com/2026-baseball). If you can't get to Nashville before the end of September you can [view all the works online](https://ziehersmith.primer.gallery/ptf18zh) and read Scott Zieher's essay [Aleatory Notes Celebrating the Baseball in Art and Poetry](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5f3d9d6ecf7f3226656f7a46/t/6a85fef630830645ad6d9f75/1787166454347/GONFALONS+FOR+THE+GRAND+OLD+GAME.pdf) (PDF).

<blockquote>
<p>We might be aware of the many peculiar binaries and quirks of baseball— the only game where the defense controls the ball. Fail 7 of 10 times and you’ll be considered for the Hall of Fame etc.</p>

<p>But the stories we find most fascinating have less to do with today’s systems or analytics and everything to do with heart, spirit and soul.</p>
</blockquote>
