I created and deployed a <a href="https://www.nicksimson.com/human.json"><code>human.json</code></a> file to my site today, after being aware of <a href="https://codeberg.org/robida/human.json">the concept</a> for a while.

> One of the problems with the internet today is that a lot of the content is AI generated. There's no way to know for sure if a site is maintained by a real human or if it's just slop. The only way to know for sure is by getting to know the authors, which usually takes time and requires developing a relationship with them through other channels, like email or social media. But what if we could expand that trust by building a web of vouches between sites?

As it turns out I'm already being vouched for in the <code>human.json</code> files of a few others, and I thought I'd reciprocate and pay it forward for some other sites I can trust are fully human authored. The authors of each site I'm vouching for have explicitly stated in some way why they don't use genAI or LLM tools for the content on their website.
