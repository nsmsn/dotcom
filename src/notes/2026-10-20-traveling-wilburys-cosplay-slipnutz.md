It's getting closer to Halloween, and I have costumes on my mind. 

I recently came across a video of a [Traveling Wilburys cosplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TKQPUqJieGM) and shot-for-shot remake of "Handle with Care", performed by Lake Street Dive. It brings a smile to my face every time I think of it.

This year is the 25th anniversary of the debut of [The Slipnutz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j5QN4S8Gg50) on <cite>Late Night with Conan O'Brien</cite>. A good milennial costume idea: grab two friends, three sweater vests and a bag of peanuts.
