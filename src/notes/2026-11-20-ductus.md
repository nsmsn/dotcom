[Ductus](https://ductus.sorenblank.com/) is a fun web app from design engineer [Soren Blank](https://sorenblank.com/). Write with your finger, mouse, pen, or trackpad. Adjust characters in the browser, and  export a <code>OTF</code> or <code>WOFF2</code> font based on your handwriting. Neat!
