Cartoon Brew presents [Exhibits A Through P: Wile E. Coyote’s Historic Case Against Acme](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/cartoon-study/coyote-vs-acme-wile-e-clips-266098.html), written by Vincent Alexander:

> Acme’s likely defense in these instances would be that Wile E. was not using the products as intended. This premise is refuted by the Acme Giant Rubber Band, which clearly specifies on the box: “perfect for tripping Road Runners.” This is negligent misrepresentation, and the product’s failure to perform as advertised caused the plaintiff serious injury. More damningly, Acme failed to even provide the Coyote with a tiny umbrella to defend himself with.
