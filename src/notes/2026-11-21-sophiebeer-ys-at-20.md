[Sophie Beer](https://www.sophie-beer.com/) posted a [wonderful comic](https://www.instagram.com/p/Df8SubyoPaA/) celebrating the 20th anniversary of Joanna Newsom's <cite>Ys</cite> album. It's a powerful testament to music and lyrics connecting us to moments throughout our life. 

The [album production credits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ys_(album)#Production) on <cite>Ys</cite> are <strong>stacked</strong>, by the way: Van Dyke Parks did the orchestra arrangements, Steve Albini was the recording engineer, Jim O'Rourke was the mixing engineer.

Sadly the only way I can link to this is to link to Instagram. Maybe Sophie gets around to publshing this on [her blog](https://www.sophie-beer.com/) too. But the comic is that good I will put aside my disdain for the Meta platform. Sophie's art made me take the time to listen to <cite>Ys</cite> again, this time with a tab open on [Genius.com](https://genius.com/albums/Joanna-newsom/Ys).
