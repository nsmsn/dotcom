# Luddite Inside

This sticker design is a parody of a logo from a well-known tech company.

<img src="/img/static/luddite.png" alt="A parody of the Intel Inside logo reading Luddite Inside" width="360" height="360" loading="lazy">

I  was influenced by <a href="/posts/2024-call-me-a-luddite.html">my reading of <i>Blood in the Machine</i> by Brian Merchant</a>, a book I still recommend. While I "work in tech" I try to avoid and refuse exploitatitve technologies as much as possible. Yes, I am talking about Large Language Models.

These are 2-inch wide vinyl stickers you can buy through my ko-fi shop. Slap 'em on your laptop or other hardware, and remember the original Luddites didn't smash their own hand looms.
